NYC civic tools

Small, open-source tools built on NYC Open Data. No accounts, no tracking, nothing stored.

311 Ghost TrackerWhich agencies and community boards close complaints without fixing anything — and where the same problem keeps getting re-reported. Re-ReportLook up a 311 request or address, see its full history, and build a report that carries that history with it. Heat & Hot Water ScoreboardBuildings and landlords with the worst heat records, by community district. Check an address before you sign. Lane Blockage MapWhere bike and bus lanes get blocked most, how often those complaints go nowhere, and the slowest bus segments.
Data refreshes nightly from NYC Open Data. Source: github.com/zyads/zohran · MIT
